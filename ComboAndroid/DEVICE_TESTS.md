## Full Wallmaster Shuffle (0.4.14)

Run on the emulator with the 0.4.14 release candidates, including co-op with two emulators on a local server; not yet on a phone. Use a spare file in Combined randomizer mode with Randomizer > Logic & access > Entrances > Wallmaster Shuffle set to Full, and no Song of Soaring among the starting items. On a Wallmaster's line in the seed's `.entrances.txt`, the text after `->` names where it should send Link; a line that crosses games names a dungeon of the other game (`MM ...` on an `OOT` Wallmaster's line, `OOT ...` on an `MM` one). Try a few seeds with the Seed field blank to find one whose line for the room you want crosses games. For each capture, note the APK checksum, the seed, that line, Link's age and form, and where he landed. Landing anywhere else, a crash, or a lost item, check or save blocks the release. Tools > Save Editor can give items, keys and souls.

1. **Menu and seeds.** In both world views the row offers None, Own Game and Full, with no sentence under it. Generate Full seeds with a child and an adult start, and with an OoT and an MM start: each one finishes; note how long each took. Each `.entrances.txt` has one line per Wallmaster, and the spoiler log lists the same routes. With the release build's headless generator, a Full seed's `entranceLayout` requires `wallmasters-full-v1`.
2. **OoT to MM, as adult.** A Forest Temple Wallmaster (`OOT Forest Temple Wallmaster West` or `... East`) whose line names an MM dungeon: a black fade, then Link stands at the start of that MM dungeon, as MM's own Link (adult with Cross-Games Age on, check 6). Then go back to OoT through the Clock Tower door: Link is adult in OoT again.
3. **OoT to MM, as child.** `OOT Bottom of the Well Wallmaster Main` on a seed where it names an MM dungeon: the same, and back in OoT Link is a child.
4. **Song of Time after arriving.** After check 2 or 3, play the Song of Time in MM: the new cycle starts where Link last entered MM, at that dungeon's entrance.
5. **MM to OoT.** One capture in Beneath the Graveyard on night 3 (`MM Beneath The Graveyard Night 3 Wallmaster`) and one in Beneath the Well (`MM Beneath The Well Wallmaster ...`), each on a line that names an OoT dungeon: Link arrives at the start of that OoT dungeon, at the age he last had in OoT. Do one after leaving OoT as a child and one after leaving it as an adult.
6. **Cross-Games Age.** Turn on Randomizer > Logic & access > Cross-Games Age for a new Full seed. An adult OoT capture to MM arrives as adult Link in MM, and an MM capture as adult Link arrives as adult in OoT; a child stays a child both ways.
7. **Saves, deaths and reloads.** After crossing each way: void out, die and continue, save and reload, and force-stop the app and load the file. Each time Link comes back at a sensible place, nothing loops, and no item or check is lost. The same Wallmaster still sends Link to the same place. Then, in the room of an OoT Wallmaster whose line names an MM dungeon, before letting it grab Link: open a chest, unlock a locked door with a small key, and pick up a small key (with Tools > Save Editor if the room has none of its own). Let the Wallmaster take Link to MM, come back to OoT through the Clock Tower door, and go back to that room: the chest is still open, the door still unlocked, the key picked up does not appear again and still counts in the key counter, and the key spent on the door is still spent. Do the same once with a save and reload in MM before coming back. In MM, in Beneath the Well, open a chest or kill the enemies of a room, then let a Wallmaster whose line names an OoT dungeon grab Link in that same visit; go back to MM through the Happy Mask Shop and to Beneath the Well before the cycle ends: the chest is still open and the room still cleared, and the clock shows the time it had when Link left.
8. **Forms and Epona.** In MM, let a Wallmaster on a line that names an OoT dungeon grab Link while he wears the Fierce Deity, Goron, Zora or Deku Mask: he arrives in OoT as himself, and back in MM all his masks are still there. As adult after an arrival in OoT, play Epona's Song in Hyrule Field: Epona comes as usual.
9. **Co-op.** Two instances in one Anchor room, on the same Full seed file: let one player be grabbed on a line that crosses games. Only that player changes games; the other keeps playing, and shared progress still syncs both ways.
10. **Master Quest.** With an OoT USA 1.0 ROM, choose Selection Only and set Spirit Temple Quest and Bottom of the Well Quest to Master Quest and Shadow Temple Quest and Ganon's Castle Quest to Vanilla. Own Game greys out with "Set Spirit Temple Quest or Bottom of the Well Quest to Vanilla to use Wallmaster Shuffle.", while Full stays offered and generates; its `.entrances.txt` has 16 Wallmaster lines.
11. **Older builds.** 0.4.13 refuses a Full seed from check 1 ("This seed cannot be used by this build"). A seed made by this build with Own Game, and one with None, still load there.
12. **Reset during a capture.** Let an OoT Wallmaster whose line names an MM dungeon grab Link, and during the black fade use Reset. Load the same file: Link is in OoT. Walk through the first door or loading zone: he stays in OoT and nothing sends him to MM. Then do the same from MM, with an MM Wallmaster whose line names an OoT dungeon: after the reset and reload, the first door or loading zone in MM keeps him in MM. It must end in `OK`. Its per-dungeon lines show the rooms seen as child and as adult; once every dungeon is done, `--require-all` must pass too. Ten MQ rooms have no actors, and their lines show `actors=mq:0`: Deku Tree 11, Dodongo's Cavern 16, Jabu-Jabu 15, Forest 22, Fire 26, Water 22, Spirit 11 and 28, Shadow 22 and Ice Cavern 10.

4. On another spare file, apply the Allsanity preset and generate: the seed view lists the dungeons that came up Master Quest, and only those play as MQ (the log's `quest=` field).

**Developer walk (DEV APK only)**

A DEV APK is built like a release with `-DCOMBO_MQ_WALK_DEV=ON` in its own build directory (BUILDING.md). `verify_apk.py` refuses it, so it is never a release; it installs over one with `adb install -r`, since both are signed with the same debug key. It loads every room of the 12 dungeons without playing them, which covers checks 1 and 2 and the room counts below, not their gameplay steps.

- Load the file of check 3 (MQ Dungeon Count 12) and stand Link in OoT on solid ground, with no menu open. Keep autosave off.
- `python tools/mq_walk_driver.py --serial <serial> walk --out <folder>`. For each dungeon it sends SoH's developer warp `entrance <hex>`, then `mq_wait <scene>`, then `mq_walk`, which loads every room through SoH's own room-load path (doors use it), the start room last, each for 20 frames, and logs `walk done scene=X rooms=N`. It then copies only the `[ComboShip MQ` lines of `native.log` from its run (no save, no other file) and runs `tools/mq_walk_compare.py --require-all` on them, which must end in `OK`. `--dungeons Forest,Spirit` walks some only, compared without `--require-all`.
- `python tools/mq_walk_driver.py --serial <serial> wallmaster <dungeon> <room> [--index N]` warps to the dungeon if Link is elsewhere and stands him where that room's Wallmaster in `mq_rooms.bin` drops (`wallmasters` lists all 16 with their commands): Forest 19 and 20, Spirit 5, 8 and 15, Bottom of the Well 0, 1 and 3, Gerudo Training Ground 2, Ganon's Castle 18. A timer or proximity Wallmaster then drops on its own; a switch one still waits for its sun or switch.
- The commands also work typed in the console window: `entrance <hex>`, `mq_wait <scene hex> [frames]`, `mq_walk [settle 1-90]`, `mq_tp <room> <x> <y> <z> [yaw]` (stands Link on the floor below the point), `mq_status`, `mq_log 0|1` (sets the room log of check 1 without saving it; a later settings change in the menu saves it too) and `mq_stop`. Over adb, the driver writes one `<id> <command>` line at a time to `ComboShip/mq-walk-commands.txt`; the game takes the file every 15 frames, only while OoT is in front, and runs only these commands from it. Its replies are `[ComboShip MQ walk] id=<id> ...` lines in `native.log`.
- A walk saves nothing. Loading a room marks it visited on the pause map (the walk puts the scene's visited rooms back when it ends), clears the temporary clear and switch flags, adds an entry to the play statistics and can note a check's item that the camera sees; the warp sets the entrance a save would start from. Do not save after a walk unless a check asks for it.

**In every dungeon**, with the log on: walk every room at each age listed, until the comparer shows all of that dungeon's rooms. Open one chest and collect one Gold Skulltula token: the item is given and the check tracker marks the check. Nothing crashes, no door is missing, and Link never falls through a floor.
