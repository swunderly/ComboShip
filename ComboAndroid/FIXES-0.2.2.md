# Combo Ship 0.2.2

This update refines the five menu changes requested after 0.2.1.

- **Matching starting inventories:** MM now uses OoT-style Equipment, Items, Songs and Other icon groups, plus a Masks group. Unselected icons are faded. Selecting an item keeps it in the same position. Progressive items have a tier picker and count indicator. The whole page scrolls together; the small top scroll box and separate available-items panel are gone.
- **Randomizer → Gameplay rules:** Gibdo trades, frog choir count, dog race results and Zora egg count live here with the other rules. The separate Randomizer Difficulty page is removed.
- **Enhancements → Difficulty → Majora's Mask:** Bremen chick count lives only here, with a 1–10 slider. It changes quest length and has no Include in logic checkbox. Existing saves and old one-chick settings retain their counts.
- **Enhancements → Difficulty → Ocarina of Time:** the former Minigames controls live here with the other difficulty settings.
- **Video & Audio → Camera:** camera controls for both games are here, including OoT's original camera panel previously inside the controller editor. Search opens their new location and keeps the selected setting visible.

Install over the previous Combo Ship APK to retain app data. No ROMs or game data are included.

## Phone checks

1. Open Starting inventory in both games. Toggle an MM mask or item, choose a progressive tier, then restart and confirm the choices remain.
2. Scroll MM's inventory and adjust Starting Hearts. The main page should scroll without a tiny separate top panel.
3. Check the new Gameplay rules, Difficulty and Camera locations above. Search for Bremen and Camera and follow the result.

Exact build and emulator checks are recorded in [VALIDATION-0.2.2.md](VALIDATION-0.2.2.md). Physical-phone touch and a complete playthrough remain outside those checks.
