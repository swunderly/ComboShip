# Combo Ship 0.2.0

Install `ComboShip-Android-0.2.0-arm64.apk` over the existing app. Its application identity and signing certificate are unchanged; keep the app installed to retain your ROMs, saves and settings.

## Menu finishing pass

- Search accepts multiple words in either order and familiar names such as cross-game travel, adult warp, Requiem, owl statues and HUD. Results are ranked by relevance and grouped by pages, shared rules, techniques and native settings.
- Every result identifies its destination and scope. Shared rule and technique links open the matching control with focus; native settings retain their real callbacks. Clear search and Back to search results make browsing predictable.
- Songs & travel groups cross-game playback, mixed destinations and any-age OoT Soaring together. Dependencies are explained with a link to the matching control rather than silently changing other options.
- First-open Randomizer and Enhancements guides explain seed logic, gameplay abilities and mid-run changes. Dismiss once; use Guide to read them again. Randomizer selections enable the supported ability and its logic. Enhancements can enable abilities without adding a new logic assumption; Include in logic is the explicit opt-in. Disabling an assumed ability can affect completion. Original seed placements are not regenerated by a menu edit.
- Long gameplay-rule labels wrap and remain tappable. Bounded quantities keep sliders, and the shared navigation retains per-page scroll positions.

## Song logic consistency

Native OoT/MM same-game song searches now read the saved logical destination mapping independently of gameplay-only toggles. The older oracle export is consistent with the explicit logical getter. Gameplay keeps its runtime destination mapping. The final native tests vary runtime and logical settings independently and verify actual engine reachability.

Adult cross-game Soaring is available under **Randomizer or Enhancements → Gameplay rules → Songs & travel**. Enable **Play warp songs across games** and **Song of Soaring at any age (OoT)**. Adult Link still needs learned Soaring, OoT's usable instrument/D-L-U buttons and an activated MM source owl. Requiem from MM requires learned Requiem and MM's usable instrument/A-D-R buttons; it does not require an OoT instrument. Soaring with no owls plays the normal performance and no-destination text.

The [completion audit](RELEASE-AUDIT-0.2.0.md) reconciles all earlier accepted requests with the completed implementations and recorded tests. Fierce Deity gap jumping remains excluded by request. [Versioned validation](VALIDATION-0.2.0.md) records final tests and device-testing limits.

## Quick checks on your phone

1. Search for `adult warp`, `cross game travel`, `Requiem`, `owl statues` and `HUD`. Open a result, return to results, and clear the query.
2. Read and dismiss both section guides. Restart, confirm they stay dismissed, and reopen them using Guide.
3. Try adult OoT Soaring with the two options enabled, then play Requiem from MM. Check native melody, confirmation, cancellation and arrival with your actual instruments/buttons/owls.
4. Confirm your previous save, tracker shortcut, touch layout and screen framing are retained.

No ROMs, extracted private game assets, saves, seeds, spoilers, logs or signing keys are included in the APK or public source ZIP.
